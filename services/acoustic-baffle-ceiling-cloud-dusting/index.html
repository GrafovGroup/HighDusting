Scope the right work

Care for the ceiling features that shape your space.

Remove loose surface dust from accessible acoustic baffles and suspended ceiling clouds in offices, restaurants, auditoriums and education buildings. Panel material, finish and suspension details guide the proposed method, with manufacturer care instructions reviewed before work.

What to include

  • Panel manufacturer, product name and care information where available
  • Baffle spacing, cloud height and photographs of accessible faces and edges
  • Furniture, AV equipment and finishes beneath the work area

Share photos, ceiling height, approximate area and operating windows. Surface compatibility, dust characteristics and access conditions determine the proposed method.

Identify the material

Share a product reference or close-up photos of the finish, edges and suspension. A method that suits one ceiling product may not suit another.

Define reachable surfaces

Distinguish vertical baffles from horizontal clouds. Specify which faces and edges can be reached without moving panels or disturbing their suspension.

Coordinate the room

Identify tables, seating, displays and AV equipment below. Plan a released work area and a cleaning window with the building team.

Scope boundaries

This service focuses on loose surface dust. Repainting, stain removal, panel replacement, suspension repairs and claims about restoring acoustic performance are outside the dusting scope.

Before you request a quote

Your scope.
Clear answers.

Use these details to prepare a facility-specific request.

Can all acoustic baffles and ceiling clouds be cleaned the same way?

No. Felt, fabric-faced, mineral-fibre and other acoustic products have different care requirements. Provide the product details if available. We assess the material and accessible surfaces against the manufacturer instructions; stain treatment, panel removal and repairs are separate scopes.

What affects the cost of this service?

The quote depends on the surfaces and extent of work, ceiling height, dust conditions, access equipment, protection requirements and available work windows. Share the facility location and photos to help define the scope; a site visit may be needed.

How do I request a quote for my facility?

Start with your name, email, city and a short description of what needs cleaning. Phone and photos are optional. We can discuss measurements, access and timing during follow-up; availability and methods are confirmed for your project.
