A few things worth knowing

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What does high dusting include?

High dusting addresses dust and cobwebs on elevated surfaces such as rafters, beams, ceiling structures, duct exteriors, pipes and fixtures. Your quote sets out exactly which surfaces and areas are included.

Can cleaning be scheduled outside our operating hours?

After-hours, weekend and shutdown windows can be considered during scoping. Tell us your preferred window and production restrictions; timing is confirmed with the service team before booking.

Do we need to provide a scissor lift?

Not necessarily. Let us know whether equipment is available on site. Reach, aisle clearance, floor conditions and equipment suitability determine whether existing or separately arranged access equipment can be used.

Can you handle combustible or hazardous dust?

Potentially combustible or hazardous dust requires a separate assessment before any cleaning method is proposed. Identify known materials in your request. Standard high dusting or HEPA vacuuming is not automatically suitable for these environments.

What information do you need for a quote?

Facility type, city, approximate area, ceiling height, surfaces to clean, dust type and available work windows are a useful starting point. Clear photos help assess the scope. Some projects also require a site visit.

Do you offer recurring or multi-location service?

You can request a one-time project, a recurring maintenance scope or coordinated work at several locations. Frequency and local availability are assessed against each facility’s needs.
