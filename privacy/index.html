Last updated September 10, 2026.

Information you provide

When you request a quote, HighDusting.ca collects the contact details, company information, facility details and optional photos or videos you choose to submit.

Form submissions

Quote details are sent through Formspree, our form-processing provider. Optional photos or videos can be emailed separately to info@highdusting.ca.

How it is used

We use this information to assess your project, contact you about the request, prepare a scope and coordinate potential service. Relevant project information may be shared with service providers involved in assessing or delivering the work. Please do not upload confidential documents or unnecessary personal information.

Storage and your choices

Requests are stored for project assessment and follow-up. You may ask about your information or request a correction or deletion by calling (888) 451 - DUST (3878). Records may need to be retained where required to administer an ongoing project or meet applicable obligations.

Website technology

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Fonts may be requested from Google Fonts, which receives standard connection information such as an IP address when your browser makes that request. The hosting service may process technical request logs to operate the website.
